#### 1. Chosen Typography

- **Standard Body Prose**: `#d4d4d4` (Body Gray)
- **Secondary / Nav**: `#a3a3a3` (Muted Gray)
- **Subtle Metadata**: `#737373` (Dim Gray)
- **Glass Translucencies**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03 / 0.05 / 0.07 / 0.09)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.

---
